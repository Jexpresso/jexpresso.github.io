Videos

Tropical clouds over 12.5 hours (GATE case) in a 60 km × 60 km domain, with adaptive mesh refinement wherever there is cloud. Run on 40 MPI ranks and two GPUs.

Atmosphere and planets

Clouds, mountain waves, rising bubbles and flows on a sphere, using the Euler and shallow water equations.

Compressible flow

Shocks and instabilities in compressible flow, with DynSGS for shock capturing.

Magnetohydrodynamics

Flows of electrically conducting fluids, where the magnetic field and the flow affect each other.

Laguerre semi-infinite elements

Semi-infinite elements that absorb waves at the edges of the domain, so they don't reflect back.

Adaptive mesh refinement

The mesh gets finer only where the flow needs it.

Solid mechanics

Elasticity of solid bodies.
